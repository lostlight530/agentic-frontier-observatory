## 1. 专业归属：这是 CS 的哪一类？ / Disciplinary Position

**中文** — 全球观察站处于**计算机科学技术调查、系统架构比较、技术成熟度分析及科技社会研究**的交汇处。其对象不是一个在本仓运行的 Agent，而是外部世界不断变化的模型、运行时、协议、硬件、授权、测试、标准、产业与使用结构。CS2023 的 AI/SEC/SE/PDC/NC 等知识领域给出对技术对象的分类，但观察、新闻确认、性能复测和社会影响分析属于不同证据层级，不能互相替代。

**English** — The Global Observatory sits at the interface of **computer-science technology surveillance, systems architecture comparison, maturity analysis, and the societal study of computing**. It does not itself host all external agent runtimes it studies. Its objects are changing models, protocols, infrastructure, identity/authority systems, evaluations, standards, and ecosystems. CS2023 provides disciplinary vocabulary; evidence about existence, announced capability, deployed operation, and societal impact must stay distinct.

## 3. 人话解释 / In Plain Language

**中文** — 好比**全球技术雷达站兼档案馆**：一家公司说新 Agent 协议发布，观察站要找原版标准、GitHub 实际版本、声明适用范围和证据缺口；它不会因为发布了官网就写“所有厂商已经互通”。

**English** — Think of a **global technology radar station with an archive**. When a vendor announces an agent protocol, the observatory identifies the exact document and release, examines public evidence, and tracks missing interoperability tests. An announcement never automatically becomes worldwide deployment.

## 4. 如何读懂而不夸大？ / Verification and Non-claim Boundaries

**中文** — `FACT`、`ATTRIBUTED_EXTERNAL_CLAIM`、`OBSERVATORY_ANALYSIS` 和 `UNCERTAIN` 是不同的研究对象。同一发行者在网站和 GitHub 重复自己的声明不构成两个独立证人。观察到某页面当前状态，不能据此反推出其状态转换发生的确切日期；研究报告存在不等于外部系统经独立运行验证。

**English** — `FACT`, `ATTRIBUTED_EXTERNAL_CLAIM`, `OBSERVATORY_ANALYSIS`, and `UNCERTAIN` refer to different epistemic states. A publisher repeating itself across a website and repository is not independent corroboration. A currently displayed status cannot establish the exact transition date; a report does not mean the external system was executed or benchmarked.

## 5. 十仓中的独特贡献 / Place in the Ten-repository Research System

**中文** — 双观察站中的**全球视角**。它为十仓内部的复杂 Agent 科研提供外部发展坐标与反例，却不自动变成其余九仓的代码要求。与中国观察站做“政策比政策、标准比标准、协议比协议、运行时比运行时”的同层比较。

**English** — It supplies the portfolio's **global external-world lens**. Its evidence can motivate questions for internal engineering repositories without silently rewriting their requirements. Comparison with China Observatory must remain same-layer: policy-to-policy, standard-to-standard, protocol-to-protocol, and runtime-to-runtime.

## 6. 纪念与升华 / Commemoration and Wider Meaning

**中文** — **技术观察的价值不在于每天宣布未来已经到来，而在于准确区分未来正在被提出、被实现、被测试，还是仍无证据。**

**English** — **The value of technological observation is not declaring tomorrow already here; it is distinguishing what has been proposed, built, tested, and what still lacks evidence.**
