# F5｜Evaluation, Safety, Governance, and Standards / 评测、安全、治理与标准

F5 tracks how AI/agent systems are measured, constrained, audited, governed, standardized, secured, and overseen.

## Core questions

- What property is an evaluation actually measuring, on which system/configuration/population?
- Is a benchmark a capability test, reliability test, safety test, preference measure, compliance check, or proxy?
- What are the evaluator's coverage, contamination, elicitation, reward-hacking, false-positive/false-negative, and evaluation-awareness limits?
- Is a governance item a proposal, draft, consultation, standard, regulation, effective requirement, implementation guide, audit result, or enforcement event?
- What security/safety issue is demonstrated, and what remains a threat model or mitigation proposal?

## Preferred evidence

Prefer original benchmark/evaluation papers and artifacts, standards bodies, regulators, official incident disclosures, technical system/safety cards, security advisories, and independent evaluation/red-team research.

A standard or regulation is primary for its own text/status. Vendor interpretations are evidence of the vendor's interpretation, not authoritative legal conclusions.

## Evaluation and governance boundaries

```text
benchmark PASS != general correctness
safety evaluation != universal safety
red-team finding != population-wide failure rate
standard published != adopted everywhere
policy announced != effective requirement
compliance claim != independent certification
incident report != universal product property
mitigation documented != mitigation effective
```

## Status discipline

Where governance maturity matters, retain the exact lifecycle state and jurisdiction/scope. Do not collapse draft, consultation, approved, published, effective, implemented, audited, and enforced states.

Where evaluation matters, retain system/version, harness, tools, safeguards, configuration, dataset, sampling, comparator, metric, result, and uncertainty.

## Handoff to F7

F5 contributes the named evaluation/governance/security object, exact property/status, scope, dates, authority, limitations, independent corroboration/counterevidence, and the narrow conclusion it supports.

F5 does not convert evaluator authority, regulatory prominence, or a green compliance artifact into universal technical truth.
