# Preserved Workstreams / 保留分任务

The workstreams are the observatory's **durable research-responsibility map**. They divide the external world into independently inspectable analytical surfaces before synthesis.

They are not a scheduler, not a Daily/Weekly/Monthly SOP, and not a promise that every subtopic changes on every observation day.

## Shared evidence contract

Every workstream keeps these dimensions separate when they materially differ:

```text
source existence
source identity
claim-specific authority
observed proposition
maturity/status
independent corroboration
observatory analysis
uncertainty / unresolved conflict
```

A vendor release note can be primary for that vendor's release, but not automatically for market dominance, independent performance, safety, or universal adoption.

Repeated use of the same source lineage across workstreams is not independent corroboration.

## Shared date discipline

Track the date that actually belongs to the claim:

- event/announcement date;
- publication/update date;
- effective/release date where applicable;
- repository observation date;
- known state-transition date only when independently supported.

Current page state does not reveal an exact historical transition timestamp by itself.

## Handoff to F7

F1–F6 hand off **bounded findings**, not pre-written conclusions. A useful handoff carries:

- proposition;
- source identity and source class;
- relevant dates;
- current maturity/status;
- evidence strength and independence;
- counterevidence/conflict;
- explicit non-claims;
- why the finding matters across workstreams.

F7 may synthesize, narrow, compare, or downgrade. It must not manufacture evidence that the owning workstream did not observe.

## Relationship to periodic research

The Daily/Weekly/Monthly research chain may use these workstreams, but the workstream definitions themselves are long-lived repository knowledge. Periodic execution rules remain in their own SOPs and reports and are intentionally not duplicated here.
