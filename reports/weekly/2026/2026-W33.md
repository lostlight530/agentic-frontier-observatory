## 1｜Carry-forward from W32 / 承接创始周

The founding week established a layered agentic network:

```text
Model
→ Harness / Runtime
→ Tool & Data Access
→ Agent-to-Agent Coordination
→ Discovery & Reachability
→ Identity & Authorization
→ Conformance, Evaluation & Observability
→ Governance & Deployment Decision
```

W33 begins with a new question: **which of these layers are becoming native platform primitives, and which remain external interoperability infrastructure?**

## 2｜Week-opening signal / 开周信号

OpenAI scheduled Atlas to stop working on 9 August 2026 while explicitly moving browser-based agentic capabilities into ChatGPT and Codex. Google had already moved computer use from a standalone Gemini 2.5 computer-use model into Gemini 3.5 Flash as a built-in tool.

This suggests a structural convergence: browser and computer action are becoming core product/model capabilities, while MCP, A2A, discovery, identity, and authorization remain the external coordination fabric around those capabilities.

## 3｜W33 priority questions / 本周优先问题

1. **Core-surface consolidation** — Do more agentic capabilities move from standalone products into general ChatGPT, Codex, Gemini, Search, and developer platforms?
2. **MCP migration evidence** — How quickly do real SDKs and deployments converge on the 2026-07-28 stateless specification?
3. **A2A conformance** — Do the TCK, Inspector, SDKs, and cross-vendor implementations produce evidence stronger than protocol compatibility claims?
4. **Discovery and identity** — How do A2A Agent Cards, DNS-AID, enterprise registries, and NIST identity/authorization work compose?
5. **Operational trust** — Can policies, approvals, escalation, audit, and revocation be expressed across agent runtimes rather than inside one vendor stack?
6. **Evaluation** — Can traces reconstruct delegation, tool use, authority propagation, recovery, and human takeover?
7. **Broad AI coverage** — Continue scanning models, multimodality, compute, scientific AI, robotics, open source, labor, and governance so the observatory does not collapse into protocol-only research.

## 4｜Working hypotheses / 本周工作假设

### H1 — Agentic capability becomes native

Standalone “agent browsers” and specialized action wrappers will increasingly be absorbed into core AI products and models.

**Falsifier:** sustained growth of independent agent surfaces with capabilities that core platforms cannot or do not absorb.

### H2 — The durable moat moves upward and outward

As computer use and tool use become common primitives, differentiation shifts toward harness reliability, enterprise context, authorization, evaluation, and workflow integration.

**Falsifier:** model capability alone continues to explain most real deployment differences.

### H3 — Protocol success requires operational evidence

MCP and A2A adoption will increasingly be judged by migration, conformance, identity, observability, and production failure handling rather than specification publication.

**Falsifier:** ecosystem adoption grows without corresponding operational tooling or trust infrastructure.
