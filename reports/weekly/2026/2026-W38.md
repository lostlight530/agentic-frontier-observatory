## 1｜Executive Weekly Judgment / 周度核心判断
Task/execution and authorization-request surfaces are more explicit than successor authority, durable-effects, rollback/remediation and recovery-completion contracts.

A2A now provides directly verified normative evidence that `TASK_STATE_AUTH_REQUIRED` signals a need for additional authorization but is not itself authorization; core A2A does not define the resulting authorization/credential's scope, representation, validity or revocation semantics, and authorization obtained for one point in a Task must not automatically authorize subsequent messages. This clarifies H38-4's boundary but does not close or independently strengthen it.

`F.748.93` remains Approved 2026-08-29, `In force (prepublished)`, with English files available 2026-09-09. `Protocol Publication != Operational Maturity`.

## 4｜Durable Baseline / 稳定基线

Four-layer model: `SECURITY != EXECUTION != DURABLE EFFECTS != RECOVERY`.
