## Saturday evidence / 周六证据

NIST NCCoE continues to treat software and AI Agent identity and authorization as an open deployment problem, including identification, authorization, auditing and non-repudiation.

Several current IETF Internet-Drafts independently expose a cross-domain trust layer around Agent systems: trust domains, gateways, delegation, revocation, provenance and audit. These remain work in progress and are not represented as a unified IETF standard.

### New H7 — Trust domains become an explicit interoperability boundary

Cross-organizational Agent interoperability will increasingly require explicit treatment of trust domains, gateways, delegation, policy interpretation, revocation and audit rather than assuming a shared transport protocol implies shared trust.

**Falsifier:** production cross-domain Agent systems reliably solve accountability, delegated scope, revocation and audit using only common transport plus local IAM, without an explicit cross-domain trust model.
