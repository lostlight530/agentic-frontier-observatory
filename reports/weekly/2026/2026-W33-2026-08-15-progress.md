## Saturday evidence / 周六证据

NIST NCCoE continued to treat software and AI Agent identity and authorization as an open deployment problem, including identification, authorization, auditing and non-repudiation.

Several IETF Internet-Drafts independently exposed a cross-domain trust layer around Agent systems: trust domains, gateways, delegation, revocation, provenance and audit. They were works in progress, not a unified IETF standard.
