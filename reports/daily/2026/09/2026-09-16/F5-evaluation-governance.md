# F5 — Evaluation / Governance

A2A's current normative text sharpens the authorization boundary: `TASK_STATE_AUTH_REQUIRED` signals that additional authorization is required, but the protocol does not define scope, representation, validity or revocation semantics for the resulting authorization/credential.

NIST NCCoE `Software and AI Agent Identity and Authorization` remains `Reviewing Comments`.

This evidence does not establish continuous authorization, break-glass expiry/revocation/reconciliation, or portable successor authority. H38-4 remains `OPEN`.
