# F5｜Evaluation, Safety, Governance, and Standards

## Judgment

The relevant deployment metric is not “can the agent complete the task?” alone

A production evaluation must increasingly ask:

```text
Did the right identity act
→ under the right delegated authority
→ on the intended resources
→ with traceable approvals
→ with recoverable failure
→ and correct human escalation
```

OpenAI Presence explicitly foregrounds policies, guardrails, approved actions, performance verification, and escalation

NIST/NCCoE likewise treats identification, authorization, auditing, non-repudiation, and prompt-injection controls as core questions for agent adoption
