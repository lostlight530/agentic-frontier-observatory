# F6｜Open Source, Industry, Economy, and Society

## Ecosystem signal

User-facing agent products may consolidate into a smaller number of core AI surfaces even while protocol and infrastructure diversity expands underneath

This creates a tension:

- consolidation can reduce user complexity
- open protocols can reduce ecosystem lock-in
- stronger native action increases the value and risk of platform-level permissions

The observatory should therefore track product consolidation and standards pluralism together rather than assuming either one wins completely
