# F3｜Compute, Data, and Infrastructure

Browser and computer use turn infrastructure into part of the agent's behavioral boundary

Important production questions include:

- credential isolation
- account/session state
- browser and desktop sandboxing
- routing and rate limits
- audit logs
- failure recovery
- human takeover

MCP's stateless 2026-07-28 core, header-based routing, caching, and authorization hardening show the same broader trend: agent infrastructure is being redesigned for ordinary production systems rather than demo-specific execution environments
