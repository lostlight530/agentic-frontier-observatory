# F1｜History, Theory, and Paradigms

## Judgment

Agency is beginning to look less like a permanent standalone product category and more like a capability that general AI platforms absorb

OpenAI's Atlas retirement on 9 August 2026 is a concrete product-boundary event: browser-based agentic capability is being brought into ChatGPT and Codex

Google's June integration of computer use into Gemini 3.5 Flash points in the same direction

## Historical interpretation

Capabilities often become less visible as they mature: search, multimodality, code execution, retrieval, and tool calling moved from separate demonstrations toward platform defaults

The relevant question is therefore not whether “agents disappear,” but which layers become invisible primitives and which remain explicit governance and interoperability layers
