# F1｜History & Theory / 历史与理论

## Finding

The first three days suggest that the agentic turn is becoming a network-formation problem layered on top of the model-capability problem

The useful historical analogy is not “agents are the new websites” but that durable ecosystems require multiple separable layers: naming, discovery, communication, identity, authority, evidence, and governance

## Boundary

Agent systems differ from ordinary network endpoints because they infer, delegate, act under uncertainty, and can reinterpret instructions. Any Internet analogy must preserve these differences rather than imply that existing network standards already solve agent trust

## Judgment

Track **layer formation** as a historical signal: when a problem moves from product-specific implementation into a reusable protocol, foundation project, standard, or common operational practice, the ecosystem has crossed a structural threshold
