# F6｜Ecosystem & Society / 生态与社会

## Finding

The Linux Foundation’s stewardship of agentic projects and DNS-AID illustrates a broader move toward vendor-neutral infrastructure

## Analysis

Neutral governance can lower adoption risk and reduce single-vendor control, but it does not automatically solve:

- semantic interoperability
- market concentration in models and cloud infrastructure
- responsibility for delegated actions
- unequal access to compute and high-quality tools
- regulatory fragmentation

The social consequence of agent infrastructure will depend on who can issue identity, delegate authority, observe actions, and revoke access—not only on who publishes an open protocol
