# F1｜History, Theory, and Paradigms

W33 supports a historical shift from treating “the agent” as a standalone product category toward treating agency as a capability embedded in general AI platforms

The counter-movement is equally important: as visible agent surfaces consolidate, identity, delegation, provenance, audit, and trust boundaries become more explicit rather than disappearing

**Durable judgment:** mature agency can become invisible at the product layer while accountable agency remains visible at the systems layer
