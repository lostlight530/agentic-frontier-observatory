# F5｜Evaluation, Safety, Governance, and Standards

W33's strongest methodological result is that specification publication cannot stand in for operational maturity

Evaluation must separate:

- identity validity
- request provenance
- delegated scope
- policy compliance
- task correctness
- recovery behavior
- revocation
- human escalation
- auditability

The current IETF Agent trust materials remain Internet-Drafts, while NIST continues to frame identity and authorization as deployment problems

**Durable doctrine:** evidence must be graded by status and by operational depth, not by how complete a document appears
