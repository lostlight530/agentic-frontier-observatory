# F6｜Open Source, Industry, Economy, and Society

W33 shows two ecosystem movements at once

User-facing agent capability is consolidating into major AI platforms, while the infrastructure needed to make those agents accountable is becoming more plural: registries, identity systems, Web request verification, delegation mechanisms, enterprise policy, and standards work

This creates a strategic tension between convenience at the surface and interoperability underneath

**Open question:** whether open trust primitives become sufficiently portable to prevent cross-platform agency from collapsing into bilateral vendor integrations
