# F1｜History, Theory, and Paradigms

## Judgment

Agent infrastructure is separating **finding a capability** from **being permitted to use it**

The historical analogy is not exact, but useful: the web developed distinct layers for naming, search, access control, and execution. ARD suggests an analogous decomposition for machine-actionable capabilities

A mature agent ecosystem therefore may not have one universal directory. It may depend on federated discovery surfaces whose answer sets are shaped by local policy and trust
