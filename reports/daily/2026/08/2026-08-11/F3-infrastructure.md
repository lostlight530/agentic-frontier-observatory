# F3｜Compute, Data, and Infrastructure

ARD elevates several previously ad hoc components into infrastructure:

- resource publication metadata
- discovery services and registries
- ranking
- federation and upstream sources
- organizational scoping
- conformance tooling

The key infrastructure risk is no longer only whether a resource endpoint is reachable. It is whether discovery results are fresh, authentic, policy-appropriate, and traceable to a trustworthy operator
