# F1 — History / Theory / Paradigm

## 2026-08-31 finding

The first observatory month ends with a conceptual correction:

> **Relationship is not authority.**

A2A can represent a contextual relation to another Task through `referenceTaskIds`, but that relation does not itself encode predecessor/supersedes semantics and does not grant permission to inspect the referenced execution.

This strengthens the August shift from model-only analysis toward stateful distributed Agent systems.
