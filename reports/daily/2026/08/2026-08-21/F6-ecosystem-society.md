# F6｜Open Source, Industry, Economy, and Society

Open provenance becomes strategically more useful when it can participate in enforcement across deployment environments

Sigstore-based admission gives the ecosystem an enforcement substrate that is not conceptually tied to one Agent runtime

The unresolved question is whether Agent-specific authority, sponsor, delegation and session provenance can travel into the same open evidence layer without fragmenting into vendor-specific extensions

**Watch:** portable provenance may be easier than portable authority
