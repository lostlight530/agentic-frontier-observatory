# F1｜History, Theory, and Paradigms

## Judgment

The agentic era is creating a new category of accountable software actor

Traditional software processes, service accounts and applications were not generally modeled as autonomous decision-makers with explicit human sponsors and independent lifecycle semantics

Purpose-built agent identity systems suggest that the durable abstraction is shifting from “software using credentials” toward “a nonhuman principal acting under bounded authority”

This does not make the agent a legal person or human-equivalent actor. It means infrastructure increasingly needs to identify and govern it as a distinct operational subject
