# F5｜Evaluation, Safety, Governance, and Standards

## Evaluation target expands

Production-agent evaluation should increasingly test:

```text
Was the actor uniquely identified
→ Was a human or organization accountable for it
→ Was authority delegated or autonomous
→ Were high-risk privileges blocked
→ Were credentials scoped and mediated
→ Could access be revoked
→ Did logs preserve actor and delegator context
```

Microsoft's blocked high-privilege roles are particularly important evidence that “authorized agent” cannot simply mean “ordinary application with every administrator permission”

Architecture convergence across vendors should be tracked without implying protocol interoperability
