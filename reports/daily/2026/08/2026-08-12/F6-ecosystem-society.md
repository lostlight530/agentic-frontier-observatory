# F6｜Open Source, Industry, Economy, and Society

## Accountability becomes organizational infrastructure

As agents act without constant human interaction, organizations need to answer who remains accountable for purpose, access reviews, incidents, retirement and orphaned identities

Microsoft's sponsor model makes this explicit at the enterprise directory layer

Google and AWS emphasize technical identity and credential controls more strongly in the reviewed sources

The social/governance question is therefore not whether responsibility transfers from humans to agents, but how human and organizational responsibility remains attached to delegated nonhuman action
