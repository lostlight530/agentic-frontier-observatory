# F4｜Agents, Runtimes, Harnesses, and Protocols

Today's key distinction is between protocol interoperability and trust interoperability

A2A-, MCP-, HTTP-, or API-level connectivity can move messages and actions across systems, but a foreign domain still needs to interpret identity, delegated scope, provenance, revocation and policy

The emerging stack is therefore:

`discovery → identity → delegation → trust-domain interpretation → negotiation / acceptance → action`
