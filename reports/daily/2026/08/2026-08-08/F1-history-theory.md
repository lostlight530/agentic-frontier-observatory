# F1｜History & Theory / 历史与理论

## Result

No paradigm break was confirmed today. The agentic turn remains best understood as a systems-integration phase layered on symbolic interfaces, statistical learning, neural representations, software engineering, and institutional control.

今天没有发现足以重写历史分期的新范式。当前智能体阶段仍然是多层历史能力的重新耦合，而不是旧范式被完全替代。

## Durable interpretation

The unit of intelligence is moving from a model output toward a system trajectory: delegation, tool use, environment interaction, recovery, and authority all affect whether a result should count as successful.
