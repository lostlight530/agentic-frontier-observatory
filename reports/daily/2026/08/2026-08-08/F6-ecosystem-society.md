# F6｜Ecosystem & Society / 生态与社会

## Result

The agent ecosystem is increasingly institutional rather than vendor-isolated.

The Linux Foundation’s Agentic AI Foundation provides neutral governance for MCP, goose, and AGENTS.md, while A2A is maintained under Linux Foundation governance by a multi-company technical steering committee.

## Analysis

Neutral governance can reduce single-vendor control, but it does not automatically prove semantic interoperability, safe deployment, or broad adoption. Governance structure and implementation evidence must be tracked separately.
