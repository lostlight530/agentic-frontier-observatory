# F6｜Open Source, Industry, Economy, and Society

Shared workspace agents change the social unit of agency

The relevant actor is no longer only one user with one assistant; it can be a reusable organizational agent with editors, owners, groups, shared connections, schedules, and API triggers

That raises accountability questions around who owns the agent, who can modify it, whose credentials it uses, and who can invoke it

**W34 observation:** organizational adoption will increasingly depend on governance ergonomics, not only raw model capability
