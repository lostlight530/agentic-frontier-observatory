# F5｜Evaluation, Safety, Governance, and Standards

W34 establishes a stronger evaluation separation

A production Agent can have:

- valid identity
- visible session evidence
- reviewed source
- valid artifact provenance
- admission-policy success

and still fail semantic correctness, business authorization or runtime safety

**Durable doctrine:** governance evidence must be graded by what it proves, what policy consumes it, and what operational consequence follows

Revocation must also be evaluated as propagation, not merely as the existence of a delete or disable signal
