# F6｜Open Source, Industry, Economy, and Society

W34 shows a tension between vendor-local governance and portable evidence

Workspace controls, Agent identity products and enterprise policy remain platform-specific, while Git/Sigstore/SLSA-style provenance can be more portable across delivery systems

**Open design pressure:** provenance portability is advancing faster than authority portability

The next question is whether open Agent ecosystems can preserve sponsor, delegated authority, policy and revocation semantics across product boundaries without collapsing into vendor-specific bilateral integrations
