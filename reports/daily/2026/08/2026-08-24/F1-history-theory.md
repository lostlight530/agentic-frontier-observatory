# F1｜History, Theory, and Paradigms

W35 begins by separating **admission-time trust** from **live runtime trust**

W33 built the trust stack; W34 showed that parts of governance can directly admit or reject execution. The next paradigm shift is temporal: trust must be revisable while a session or workload is already active

**Opening judgment:** mature autonomous systems will require trust decisions that can change over time rather than a one-time pass/fail at launch
