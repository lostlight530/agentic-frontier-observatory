# F6｜Open Source, Industry, Economy, and Society

As autonomous execution becomes more persistent, exception handling becomes part of governance infrastructure

Break-glass access, temporary policy bypasses, delegated handoffs, employee departure, incident rollback, and non-code outputs all create lifecycle states that cannot be represented by a single allow/deny decision

**W35 question:** do product ecosystems expose owner, scope, expiry, reason, audit, and revocation semantics for these exceptions?
