## 5｜Runtime and Contract Analysis / Runtime 与 Contract 边界
Poppy 0.4 and Agent Substrate 1.0 are useful to compare as contract and runtime layers, but no evidence links the projects into one interoperable stack

- Session-scoped tasks endpoint != persistent task store
- at-most-once specification text != verified exactly-once external effect
- actor snapshot/restore != durable external-effect recovery
- SPIFFE workload identity != end-user delegation or policy approval
- sandbox isolation != authorization decision
- GKE GA != cross-cloud interoperability
- CNCF Sandbox != Incubating or Graduated
- benchmark mean reward != accuracy
- wet-lab data interpretation != physical instrument execution
